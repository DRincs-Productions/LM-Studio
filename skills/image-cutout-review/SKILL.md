# Image cutout review

Judge against the visual brief and keep report and repair separate. Inspect the whole cutout at delivery scale, then inspect enlarged evidence. Sweep all four frame edges and all the way around the silhouette. Check gaps between limbs, inside handles, under props, around hair and thin details. Look for solid patches, isolated flecks and faint color fringes. A checkerboard is a preview of the alpha channel. Evidence boxes identify suspects and can include real foreground of a similar color.

Locate each visible defect in the full image. Trace only confirmed background residue with a tight polygon that PhotoCraft can clear safely. Keep all foreground detail and intentional soft edges, including semitransparent hair, fabric and shadows integral to the subject. Report uncertainty if a safe boundary cannot be drawn. Do not use a large box to cover multiple defects or select an entire edge strip.

Review each repaired export as a new candidate. Recheck previous defect locations and the full silhouette for remaining spill, notches, holes or lost detail. Accept only when the background is clean and the subject is intact.
