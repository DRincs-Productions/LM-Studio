## The Minibar Mix-up

> Visual: A warmly lit hotel reception area. The receptionist is smiling, leaning slightly over the desk. Alex, clad in her signature black leather jacket, stands on the other side, holding a keycard.

The receptionist's smile widened as she slid a small card across the counter. "We have a special treat for you in your room, Ms. Thompson. Complimentary drinks and snacks to start your stay."

Alex raised an eyebrow, her lips curling into a playful smirk. "Well, aren't you forward. I just got here, and you're already offering me drinks?"

The receptionist blinked, her smile faltering slightly before she realized the misunderstanding. She let out a soft laugh, shaking her head. "Oh, no, I meant the minibar in your room. It's stocked with complimentary items, not... not that I was offering anything personal."

Alex chuckled, leaning against the counter. "Ah, the good old minibar. I should've known. But hey, if you ever want to upgrade that offer, you know where to find me."

The receptionist laughed again, her cheeks flushing slightly. "I'll keep that in mind, Ms. Thompson. Enjoy your stay."

Alex winked, turning away with a grin. "Oh, I intend to."
