# Scene: The Minibar Mix-up

## Narrative

> Visual: A warmly lit hotel reception area. The receptionist is smiling, leaning slightly over the desk. Alex, clad in his signature black leather jacket, stands on the other side, holding a keycard.

**Receptionist:** We have a little surprise for you in your room, Alex. Complimentary drinks and snacks to enjoy during your stay.

**Alex:** Well, well, looks like I hit the jackpot. You're not usually this generous with the welcome drinks, are you?

**Receptionist:** *blinking* Oh, I see how this might sound. I meant the minibar in your room is stocked with complimentary items. It's not a personal offer; it's part of our hotel service.

**Alex:** *chuckling* Ah, the minibar. Should've known. But hey, if you ever want to upgrade that offer to something more personal, you know where to find me.

**Receptionist:** *laughing softly* I'll keep that in mind, Alex. Enjoy your stay.

**Alex:** *winking* Oh, I fully intend to. Thanks for the... minibar.

## Italian Summary
In questa scena, Alex scambia per un'offerta romantica il commento della receptionist riguardo ai drink e agli snack del minibar in camera. Alex risponde con umorismo e un po' di malizia, ma la receptionist chiarisce subito che si tratta solo di un servizio dell'hotel. La scena mantiene un tono giocoso e leggermente malizioso, in linea con i personaggi e il tono stabilito.
