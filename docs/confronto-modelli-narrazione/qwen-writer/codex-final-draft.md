# Scene: Hotel Reception

## Narrative

> Visual: Hotel reception area – modern but not luxurious. A tall potted plant in the corner, the faint hum of a mini-fridge under the counter. The receptionist (mid-20s, neat bun, polite smile) looks up from her computer as Alex approaches.

**Receptionist:** Good evening. Can I help you?

**Alex:** Hi. I just have a quick question—do y'all provide room service?

**Receptionist:** Room service until 10 p.m., yes. But if you just want a drink or a light snack, you can use the minibar in your room.

**Alex:** Got it. And… is that all?

**Receptionist:** That's all. I'm not saying anything more than that, you know?

Alex blinks, then grins. **Alex:** Whoa, sir… Are you trying to hit on me?

**Receptionist:** …No, I'm not hitting on you. I'm just telling you about the drinks and snacks in the minibar. That's all.

Alex leans in slightly. **Alex:** Come on, don't be shy. If you want to check me out more closely, I won't complain.

The receptionist stares, then speaks slowly and clearly. **Receptionist:** Sir… I'm just informing you that the minibar has drinks and snacks. That's all. This is not a personal invitation; it's hotel service.

Alex laughs. **Alex:** Got it, got it. So what you're saying is… you're not interested in me at all, right?

**Receptionist:** …Can I help you with anything else?

Alex smiles broadly. **Alex:** No, that's all I needed. Thanks!

## Italian Summary
Scena d'apertura al banco reception: il protagonista chiede informazioni sul room service, la receptionist le spiega l'uso del minibar in camera. Nasce un divertente fraintendimento su una possibile flirt, che viene chiarito con naturalezza e mantiene un tono leggero.
