## Hotel Reception Scene

> Visual: A cozy hotel reception area, late evening. Warm lighting, a polished wooden counter, and shelves of books in the background. The receptionist, an attractive woman in her late 20s with a friendly smile, looks up as Alex approaches.

**Receptionist:** "Can I help you?"

**Alex:** "I'd like a beer and some peanuts."

**Receptionist:** "Are you hungry?"

**Alex:** "Not really... I just feel like having a drink."

**Receptionist:** "Me too," she says with a suggestive smile. "I could have a drink with you."

**Alex:** "Really? Are you sure?"

**Receptionist:** "What do you mean 'sure'? Of course I'm sure. I said it, didn't I?"

**Alex:** "I thought you were joking."

**Receptionist:** "Why would I joke about something like that?"

**Alex:** "I don't know... just seems like the kind of thing to joke about."

**Receptionist:** "No, I'm not joking. I want a drink too."

**Alex:** "Uh... but aren't you working?"

**Receptionist:** "I am working—this is my job. I'm the receptionist here."

**Alex:** "Uh-huh... but if I ask for a drink, won't that cause problems for you?"

**Receptionist:** "No, it won't. I'm not talking about me having a drink with you. I mean the drinks and snacks in the minibar in your room. If you want something from there, just let me know. I can bring it to you myself."

**Alex:** "Oh... you mean that? Why didn't you say so from the start?"

**Receptionist:** "Didn't I make myself clear?"

**Alex:** "Not really. You led me to think you were offering something more personal."

**Receptionist:** "What are you talking about? I'm just doing my job. I'm not gonna do anything 'personal' with a customer."

**Alex:** "Got it. No problem."
