# Minibar Chemistry

## Narrative

> Visual: Hotel reception at night, warm brass lamp light, a woman behind a marble counter with a half-peeled orange and a brass bell, Alex leaning on the counter in a black leather jacket, blonde hair loose.

Alex pressed her hip against the marble counter and studied the woman behind it the way she studied menus: with intent, and without commitment.

**Receptionist:** You can have whatever you like from the minibar. Drinks, snacks, the chocolate, the little bread rolls. On the house.

**Alex:** Oh? On the house tonight? That's awfully generous. For me specifically, or for whoever happens to walk in?

**Receptionist:** You're my first guest since four o'clock, so, yes. Specifically for you.

Alex let the silence do its work, then bit her lip in a way she had once seen advertised as "effortlessly charming."

**Alex:** I take it you like me.

**Receptionist:** I like that you're staying. The minibar, specifically. There are seventeen bottles of cheap wine and four tins of biscuits in there, and the manager is a man who does not enjoy the word "biscuit" being said near him.

**Alex:** So it's a business decision.

**Receptionist:** It is a business decision. Not a personal one. I have never once flirted with a guest over bread rolls, and I am not about to start tonight, but the rolls are included, and so is the Prosecco, and that is not because I find you attractive.

Alex picked up the brass bell and gave it a tentative, testing ring. It rang with a cheerful, optimistic note that suited her.

**Alex:** Good to know. I'd hate to feel chosen for the wrong reasons.

She dropped the bell back down, straightened her leather jacket, and slid a fingertip across the counter's marble grain like she was signing something.

**Alex:** So. Is the Prosecco chilled, or do I need to put in work for it?

**Receptionist:** Chilled. The bread rolls are not.

**Alex:** Then I'll take my chances with the wine.

## Italian Summary

Scena unica alla reception dell'hotel, tono giocoso e lievemente flirtatioso. La receptionist offre ad Alex i drink e gli snack del minibar "in omaggio", e Alex, con la sua abitudine a leggere tutto come un invito, risponde come se stesse ricevendo una dichiarazione di interesse personale. La receptionist precisa chiaramente che si tratta solo del minibar, una decisione di business, non un flirt. Payoff comico: Alex accetta con grazia e chiede se il Prosecco è freddo.
