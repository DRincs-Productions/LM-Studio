# Hotel Minibar

## Narrative

> Visual: A sunlit hotel reception desk, brass bell, a single orchid in a vase. The receptionist in a navy uniform behind the counter. Alex leaning on the desk in a black leather jacket, blonde hair tousled, amused grin.

Alex dropped her bag on the luggage rack with enough enthusiasm to startle the orchid.

**Receptionist:** "Welcome to the Marlowe. Room 412 is ready. You'll find complimentary drinks and snacks."

She said it in the smooth, practiced cadence of a woman who had delivered that sentence to ten thousand guests. But Alex had been sleeping badly, traveling worse, and she heard the words the way a tired person hears everything: with extra meaning.

**Alex:** "You're offering me things? Personally?"

She slid a hand along the counter toward the little card reader, not quite touching it.

**Receptionist:** "I'm offering you a room."

**Alex:** "With snacks. And drinks. The kind of snacks and drinks that suggest someone wants to get to know you."

The receptionist looked down at her clipboard as though it had personally betrayed her. A young guest came up from the side, holding a key card, and the silence between them was suddenly very noticeable.

**Receptionist:** "I should be clear. The complimentary drinks and snacks are in the minibar in your room. They are not a personal offer. They are not a romantic offer. They are a perk of the rate."

She said each word with the precision of someone dismantling a bomb.

**Alex:** "In that case, I'd better go up and taste them before you take them back. A perk like that shouldn't be wasted on a woman who just spent three hours in a train station."

She picked up her bag, already moving toward the elevator.

**Receptionist:** "Your key card."

**Alex:** "I know." She paused, turned, and gave her a lazy, deliberate smile over her shoulder. "You're very considerate about the drinks. I'll keep that in mind."

The receptionist watched her go, then wrote something on her clipboard that was almost certainly not about the minibar.
