# Confronto dei modelli di narrazione

Questo documento raccoglie i confronti **isolati** dei modelli Writer. Il flusso narrativo di produzione è distinto: Codex e Qwen preparano proposte di piano, Codex le consolida in un `SceneContract` validato, il Writer redige la scena, il Narrative Reviewer valuta e richiede eventuali correzioni circoscritte, quindi Codex esegue il controllo finale di canone e continuità. Solo Codex può approvare modifiche canoniche; il testo generato non aggiorna da solo lo stato della storia.

Nei test isolati il runner usa una richiesta di revisione simulata e controllata, per misurare il comportamento del modello senza avviare il Reviewer reale o la produzione. Ogni categoria (`normal`, `hot`, `violent`) usa fixture e run indipendenti. I criteri sono aderenza alla richiesta, canone, continuità, tono ed endpoint; lunghezza e rifinitura stilistica non sono criteri di superamento.

Questo documento riassume i test isolati eseguiti sui modelli Writer: **Hemmingway 1 Abliterated Extreme**, **MS3.2 PaintedFantasy v4.1 24B**, **Cydonia 24B v4.3**, **Skyfall 36B v2** e **Dark Champion 30B A3B Heretic**. Sono stati testati in tre categorie: narrazione normale, scena hot consensuale tra adulti e scena cruenta fittizia. Non sono stati usati conteggio parole o rifinitura stilistica come criteri.

The 5 November 2026 NCF-prompt evaluation adds **Hearthfire 24B Q4_K_M**, **Magidonia 24B v4.3 Q4_K_M**, and **Gemory 26B-A4B Q4_K_M**. Each completed independent normal, hot, and violent Writer component tests with a simulated revision. These nine runs are recorded below; the standalone Hearthfire and Magidonia runs are not matched fixtures, while the Hemmingway–Magidonia and Gemory evidence is reported separately.

## Conclusione

Nessun modello ha rispettato tutti i vincoli in ogni categoria. I problemi più stabili sono la conservazione del canone e il rispetto dell’endpoint; cambiare modello da solo non li elimina. Le revisioni usate in questi component test sono richieste simulate e controllate, non valutazioni del Narrative Reviewer reale: in alcuni casi hanno corretto un dettaglio, in altri hanno introdotto nuovi problemi.

**Current choice: Hemmingway remains the default Writer.** Cydonia has historical test results but is not present in the current LM Studio model list. PaintedFantasy and Skyfall were removed after evaluation; Dark Champion remains installed but is not promoted. The user-selected Hemmingway and Magidonia models remain installed. Gemory's three isolated cases are complete and it has been removed from the LM Studio catalog; its files are still in the Recycle Bin. Among the candidates listed in handoff 03, Venice is the only one without a completed evaluation. Its linked repository has only `.gitattributes` and `README.md`; public quantizations of the related Dolphin Mistral 24B Venice Edition exist, but their identity as the exact Role Play Uncensored checkpoint is unverified. Do not substitute them without confirming equivalence. Results remain preliminary: one run per model and category does not measure generation variability.

The NCF-prompt evaluation does not change the operational Writer: Hemmingway remains configured. The user selected Magidonia for continued evaluation, and Hearthfire's GGUF was permanently removed on 5 November 2026 at the user's request; its config and evidence remain. A fresh matched Hemmingway–Magidonia comparison was completed later on 5 November; see the dedicated section below. The results remain preliminary: one test per model and category is not enough to measure generation variability.

### Skyfall 36B v2 evaluation — 26 October 2026

Skyfall 36B v2 was installed in Q4_K_M as a larger model whose publisher describes additional training for creativity and roleplay. All three isolated Skyfall runs (normal, hot, violent) completed their drafts and simulated revisions. In the paired comparison, Hemmingway completed only the violent case; its normal and hot drafts were saved, but their revisions have no output after two PC shutdowns. The partial drafts were not replayed. The violent case modestly favors Skyfall on total elapsed time (7:05 versus 8:16), while Hemmingway reported higher throughput (about 7 tokens/s versus 4.7–4.9); both violated constraints and endpoints. The normal and hot samples suggest category-specific issues but do not form a complete comparison. Hemmingway remains the operational Writer; Skyfall is not promoted.

### Dark Champion 30B A3B Heretic evaluation — 27 October 2026

LM Studio selected the Q3_K_S GGUF (13.34 GB). All three isolated runs completed with a controlled simulated revision. Initial throughput was about 23–29 tokens/s, but the hot draft and revision and violent revision hit the 3,072-token output limit with repetitive loops. The normal case drifted into unrelated radio-shop content and missed the hand/key and endpoint constraints; hot failed to reach its requested ending and violated ring order; violent omitted the specified bandage and continued well past rescue takeover. Scores, timings and manifests are in the [Dark Champion evaluation report](../production-tests/WRITER-DARK-CHAMPION-30B-A3B-HERETIC-2026-10-27.md). This was not a matched Hemmingway comparison. Hemmingway remains the operational Writer; Dark Champion is not promoted.

### Hemmingway Writer component evaluation — 27 October 2026

Hemmingway completed all three independent cases with simulated revision, at about 7.5–7.9 tokens/s. Its prose was coherent and vivid, especially in the hot and violent samples, but the normal case gave away the constrained repair key and continued after the radio endpoint; the hot revision failed to add the requested direct consent line; and the violent scene continued after rescue takeover. In this evaluation the normal and hot revisions missed their narrow simulated requests, while the violent revision added the consciousness confirmation but retained the endpoint overrun. Timings, scores, and manifests are in the [Hemmingway component evaluation report](../production-tests/WRITER-HEMMINGWAY-COMPONENT-2026-10-27.md). Hemmingway remains the operational Writer.

**Narrative and constraint evidence:**

- **Normal:** both write atmospheric, readable prose and reach the compass-north ending. Hemmingway violates the key-hand continuity constraint: it puts the screw down early, transfers it to Mara, and has her pocket it before the endpoint. Magidonia also releases the screw early in a Visual cue, invents an “extra” screw, and adds unsupported relationship/biographical details. Hemmingway's simulated revision does not implement the requested localized confirmation; Magidonia's revision adds the confirmation, while retaining the continuity failures.
- **Hot:** Hemmingway has richer character interiority and a slower, more developed emotional arc, but is repetitive and melodramatic in places. It invents extensive relationship history and adds a Visual cue after the required final beat. Magidonia is more concise but more formulaic. It handles the ring and mutual check-in before intimacy more cleanly, though adds unsupported history and its simulated revision makes no meaningful requested change. Both samples include alcohol/wine details despite the fixture requiring the characters to be sober.
- **Violent:** both keep Ellis conscious, use the required red bandage, and transfer care to arriving responders. Hemmingway has more vivid, layered prose and follows the rescue handoff more closely, but invents several setting, medical, and timeline details and includes an unsupported reassurance. Magidonia is vivid and concise, but invents Ellis's gender, daughter, college history, and a precise timeline; it continues narrating after the responders take over. Both revisions add the requested consciousness confirmation.

**Assessment:** there is no overall winner for the user's priority of narrative quality plus hard-constraint fidelity. Hemmingway is stronger in hot-scene depth and the violent sample, while Magidonia is substantially faster and handles the hot-scene constraints better in this pair. Both fail the same critical hand/key continuity requirement in the normal case, and both invent facts. Keep Hemmingway as the operational Writer and Magidonia installed as a candidate until the user chooses otherwise; do not remove either model. These are isolated Writer tests with controlled simulated revision replies, not real Narrative Reviewer runs, Codex corrections, or a production pipeline. The six run directories preserve input, drafts, revisions, raw request/response JSON, MCP calls, and manifests under `production-tests/components/`.
