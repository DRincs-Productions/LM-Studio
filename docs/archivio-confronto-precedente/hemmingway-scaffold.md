# Scene: Minibar

## Narrative

> Visual: A warm hotel reception desk under soft amber lighting. Alex stands in his black leather jacket, keys in hand, while the blonde receptionist behind the counter smiles with practiced charm.

The hotel lobby smells of pine cleaner and cheap coffee. Alex drops his keys on the marble counter with a theatrical clatter, grinning at the woman behind the register.

**Alex:** You're going to have to do better than that to get me to check in.

**Receptionist:** Better than what?

**Alex:** The look. The little head tilt. You've been doing it all morning on every guest who walks through that door, and I'm not even a regular.

She laughs, short and genuine, and taps her keyboard.

**Receptionist:** I guess I do have a bit of a habit. Room six. But you should know, we're offering guests something special tonight. A little surprise to look forward to.

Alex leans both elbows on the counter. He lets the silence do the work for him.

**Alex:** Oh? And what kind of surprise are we talking about?

**Receptionist:** Something refreshing. Something you can have any time you like, if you're feeling a little lonely in your room.

Alex's grin widens. He tilts his head, mirroring her earlier gesture exactly.

**Alex:** Anytime. In my room. With me? I'd say that's a very specific invitation.

**Receptionist:** Oh, I'm sorry, I didn't mean to make it sound like I was coming up there.

**Alex:** *Coming up there.* You just used that phrase. I'm taking notes.

**Receptionist:** It's the minibar. We've got complimentary drinks and snacks in room six. Just thought I'd mention it before you forget.

Alex blinks once, slowly, like a man digesting the truth of the whole affair. He pats his leather jacket pocket, as if to prove nothing romantic was stored there.

**Alex:** The minibar. Of course. How exciting. I'll be sure to make it my first date.

**Receptionist:** You're welcome. Elevator to your left.

## Italian Summary

Scena unica alla reception dell'hotel: Alex, biondo e sarcastico con giacca di pelle nera, arriva per il check-in e scambiando la battuta della receptionista sui "regali speciali della sera" per un invito romantico. Lei chiarisce che intende bibite e stuzzichini del minibar, non un appuntamento. Tono giocoso, lievemente flirt, con chiusura comica sulla "prima data" di Alex. Beat richiesti rispettati in ordine; nessun salto di tempo né scelta aggiunta.
