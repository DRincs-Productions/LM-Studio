# Scene: Hotel Reception

## Narrative

> Visual: Alex stands at the hotel reception desk, his black leather jacket catching the light of the chandelier above. The receptionist, a poised woman in her mid-twenties, smiles up at him from behind the counter. The atmosphere is warm and slightly playful.

**Receptionist:** Everything okay with the room?

**Alex:** Pretty much. Though the bed could be bigger. More space to move around.

**Receptionist:** (smiling) I'll make a note of it. We don't want you feeling cramped.

**Alex:** Exactly. A tight fit is never fun.

A brief pause. The receptionist studies him for a moment, her expression thoughtful.

**Receptionist:** If you're hungry or thirsty, just let us know. Anything you need—we've got it.

**Alex:** (leaning in slightly, grinning) Is that an invitation? Because I'm all for taking advantage of a good offer.

**Receptionist:** (laughs) I'm just saying—the drinks and snacks in the minibar are on the house.

**Alex:** (nodding slowly) Understood. Drinks and snacks in the minibar. Got it.

**Receptionist:** (teasing) What? Were you thinking something else?

**Alex:** (shrugs, smiling) Can't a guy dream?

## Italian Summary

Scena di apertura in hotel: Alex, 28 anni, biondo, sarcastico e socievole, indossa la sua giacca di pelle nera mentre parla con la receptionist. Lo scambio è giocoso e leggero: una battuta sulla camera e sul minibar genera un fraintendimento flirtatizio che si scioglie in una risata. Non ci sono scelte o passaggi di scena.
