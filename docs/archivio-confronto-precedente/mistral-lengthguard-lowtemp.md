# Scene: Minibar Mischief
## Narrative
> Visual: The hotel lobby is warm and inviting, with soft lighting casting gentle shadows. The reception desk, made of polished mahogany, sits in the center. Behind it, a receptionist with a friendly smile and neatly styled hair greets guests. Alex, leaning casually against the desk, his black leather jacket creasing slightly, grins at her.

**Receptionist:** "We have a special welcome package for our guests. Would you like to indulge in something... refreshing?"

Alex's eyes sparkle with mischief as he raises an eyebrow. "Oh, I do love a good indulgence. Especially when it's refreshing. What did you have in mind?"

The receptionist chuckles, shaking her head slightly. "I meant the minibar in your room. Complimentary drinks and snacks, of course."

Alex clutches his chest dramatically. "And here I was, picturing a private tour of the city's nightlife. Or perhaps a moonlight stroll on the beach." He sighs exaggeratedly. "But I suppose I can settle for a tiny bottle of whiskey and a packet of nuts."

The receptionist laughs, her eyes twinkling. "I'm sure the minibar will be a hit. Let me know if you need anything else."

Alex winks. "Oh, I'll be sure to do that. You can count on me."

## Italian Summary
La scena si svolge nella hall di un hotel, dove Alex, un uomo socievole e sarcastico con una giacca di pelle nera, interagisce con la receptionist. La conversazione inizia con un malinteso romantico, che si risolve in una battuta giocosa quando la receptionist chiarisce che si riferiva al minibar della stanza. Alex risponde con umorismo, mantenendo un tono leggermente malizioso. La scena è unica e continua, senza salti temporali o scelte.
