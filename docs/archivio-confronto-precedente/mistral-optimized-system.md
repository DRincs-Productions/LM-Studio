# Scene: A Drink and a Smile

## Narrative

> Visual: The hotel reception area is softly lit, the air filled with a quiet hum of conversation and the occasional clink of ice in a glass. Behind the polished desk, an adult woman with a professional smile adjusts her glasses, her fingers resting lightly on the keyboard of her computer. Alex leans against the counter, his black leather jacket creasing slightly under his relaxed posture.

**Receptionist:** "We have a special welcome package for our guests. Complimentary drinks and snacks to make your stay more enjoyable."

Alex's eyes sparkle with mischief as he raises an eyebrow. "Is that so? And here I thought you were just trying to get me alone."

The receptionist blushes slightly, a small laugh escaping her lips. "Oh, I—I meant the minibar in your room. We stock it with a few items to help you settle in."

Alex grins, not missing a beat. "Well, that's a shame. I was already imagining a much more... personal welcome."

She shakes her head, smiling despite herself. "I'm sure your room will be plenty comfortable. The minibar has everything you need."

Alex leans in slightly, his tone playful. "Everything, huh? That's quite a claim. I'll have to inspect it thoroughly."

The receptionist chuckles, her professional demeanor returning. "I'm sure you'll find it adequate. If you need anything else, just let us know."

Alex straightens up, giving her a wink. "Oh, I'll be sure to do that."

## Italian Summary
La scena rappresenta uno scambio scherzoso e leggermente malizioso tra Alex e la receptionist dell'hotel. Alex, vestito con la sua giacca di pelle nera, interpreta in modo giocoso un commento della receptionist come un'offerta romantica, solo per scoprire che si riferiva ai drink e agli snack del minibar della stanza. La scena mantiene un tono divertente e leggermente flirt, rispettando i dettagli canonici dei personaggi.
