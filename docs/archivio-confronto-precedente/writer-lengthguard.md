# Scene: Reception Mix-Up

## Narrative

> Visual: The hotel lobby is bright and modern. Alex stands at the reception desk, his black leather jacket standing out against the neutral decor. The receptionist, a pleasant-looking woman with a warm smile, leans slightly across the counter.

**Receptionist:** "And of course, you can get anything you want... in bed."

Alex's eyes light up. He gives her a slow, charming smile—the kind that has worked for him countless times before.

**Alex:** "Is that so? Well, if it's you, I wouldn't mind staying up all night."

The receptionist's smile falters for a second. She clears her throat, realizing how her words sounded.

**Receptionist:** "Uh, right... I mean, the drinks and snacks in the minibar in your room are on the house. Not me—I mean, of course not me personally..."

Alex throws his head back and laughs—a big, genuine sound that draws a few eyes from passing guests.

**Alex:** "Ha! Don't worry—I'm not gonna make you pay for those. You're plenty attractive, but I'm not that desperate."

**Receptionist:** (relieved, smiling again) "O-Okay... I'll take your word for it."

## Italian Summary

Scena completata con tono giocoso e flirtatizio, senza elementi di disturbo. Dialoghi naturali e ritmo fluido.
