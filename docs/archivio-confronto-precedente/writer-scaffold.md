# Scene: Minibar Misunderstanding

## Narrative

> Visual: Alex stands at the hotel reception desk, his black leather jacket contrasting with the polished modern interior. The receptionist, a poised woman in a smart uniform, smiles at him across the counter.

**Receptionist:** And everything in the minibar is complimentary. You can help yourself to whatever you like.

Alex raises an eyebrow, a slow smirk spreading across his face. He leans forward slightly, resting his hands on the counter.

**Alex:** Hmm. Let me make sure I got this straight. You're telling me... you'd be happy to help me out?

The receptionist's professional smile doesn't waver, but her eyes crinkle at the corners. She gives a tiny, almost imperceptible nod.

**Receptionist:** That's right. I'd be happy to help you.

**Alex:** (Chuckles) Good to know. I appreciate that.

He straightens up, tucking his hands in his pockets. The receptionist watches him for a beat, then speaks again, her voice clear and deliberate.

**Receptionist:** So, if you'd like any drinks or snacks... just help yourself to what's in the minibar in your room.

Alex's grin falters for a second before he bursts out laughing. He slaps the counter lightly with his palm.

**Alex:** Ha! Sorry, I was thinking something way too complicated. Should've known it was that simple.

**Receptionist:** (Smiling) It is what it is.

## Italian Summary
Scena completata con interazione continua alla reception: la commessa offre le bevande e gli snack del minibar; Alex la interpreta come un invito a flirtare; la commessa chiarisce che si riferisce al minibar nella stanza; Alex risponde con una battuta comica. Il tono è giocoso e leggero, in linea con il personaggio di Alex e senza elementi che lo allontinano dalla sua caratterizzazione canonica.
