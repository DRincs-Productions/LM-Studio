# Scene: Reception Mix-Up

## Narrative

> Visual: A modern hotel lobby, late afternoon. Warm light from a chandelier reflects off the polished marble floor. Alex stands at the reception desk, his black leather jacket contrasting with the formal setting. The receptionist, a woman in a neat uniform, smiles as she speaks to him. The faint smell of coffee hangs in the air.

**Receptionist:** "And of course, we're here to serve you in any way we can during your stay."

Alex gives her a slow, confident smile, his blue eyes crinkling at the corners.

**Alex:** "You know," he says, drawing out the words, "I usually don't turn down a direct offer like that."

**Receptionist:** (tilting her head, a playful glint in her eye) "Oh? And what would that be?"

**Alex:** (leaning forward slightly on the counter) "Skipping the small talk and getting straight to the point. I like that."

**Receptionist:** (laughs lightly) "Oh, you misunderstood. I was talking about the drinks and snacks in the room minibar. Not… a personal offer."

The clarification lands with perfect clarity. Alex straightens up, feigning a look of deep disappointment.

**Alex:** "Ah. You mean there's no extra charge for the beverages and food in the room?"

**Receptionist:** (smiling broadly) "That's exactly what I mean."

**Alex:** "Too bad," he says, shrugging with a grin. "I was thinking I'd take you up on it."

## Italian Summary
La scena si svolge alla reception dell'hotel. La réceptionniste dice ad Alex che l'hotel è a sua completa disposizione, frase che lui interpreta come un invito a flirtare. Lui risponde in modo ambiguo, facendo pensare che stia accettando un invito. Lei chiarisce subito che si riferisce al minibar in camera, non a una relazione personale. Alex conclude con una battuta, dicendo che è un peccato perché era interessato. Il tono è giocoso e leggero, come richiesto.
