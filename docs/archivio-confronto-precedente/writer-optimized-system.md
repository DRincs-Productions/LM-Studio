# Scene: Minibar Mix-Up

## Narrative

> Visual: A sleek hotel reception desk in the evening. Warm lighting, the soft chime of a door in the background. Alex stands at the counter, his black leather jacket catching the light. The receptionist, a poised woman with a friendly smile, looks up from her computer.

**Receptionist:** And everything in the minibar is on the house. For you.

Alex's eyes light up. He leans forward slightly, a confident smirk playing on his lips.

**Alex:** I appreciate that. But I'm not one of those guys who immediately jumps when a beautiful woman offers me something "on the house."

**Receptionist:** (confused) Huh?

**Alex:** What I'm saying is... I don't just cling to every skirt that waves in front of me.

**Receptionist:** (blinking) Uh-huh... Look, sir, I'm just telling you the hotel policy. If you get carried away and end up in bed with every guest who stops by the front desk, that's your problem.

**Alex:** (laughing) Whoa, are you jealous?

**Receptionist:** Jealous? Of what?

**Alex:** Of me, obviously.

**Receptionist:** (realizing the misunderstanding) Wait a minute—no, believe me, I'm not that cheap. I'm just informing you that the drinks and snacks in your room are complimentary. That's all I'm saying.

**Alex:** (grinning) Got it. So basically, you're telling me to help myself... right?

**Receptionist:** (firmly) Correct. Exactly.

**Alex:** Understood. I will... take that as a personal invitation.

**Receptionist:** (sighing, but smiling) Sir, please don't get me wrong. I'm just doing my job.

## Italian Summary
Scena al banco reception dell'hotel. La réceptionnist dice ad Alex che le bevande e gli stuzzichini del minibar in camera sono "di casa". Lui la interpreta come un complimento personale; lei chiarisce che si tratta solo di policy dell'hotel, senza secondi fini. Scambio giocoso e comico, senza inventare dettagli extra.
