# Scene: Minibar Mix-Up

## Narrative
> Visual: The hotel lobby is warmly lit, with soft jazz playing in the background. The reception desk is made of polished wood, and behind it stands a friendly-faced woman in her late twenties, her name tag reading "Maria." Alex leans casually against the desk, his black leather jacket creaking slightly as he shifts his weight.

**Maria:** Welcome to the Grand Hotel. I trust your room will be to your liking? We do offer complimentary drinks and snacks in the minibar, so you can relax and make yourself at home.

Alex raises an eyebrow, a smirk playing on his lips.

**Alex:** Complimentary, huh? That's quite the welcome. I must say, I'm flattered. You really know how to make a guy feel special.

Maria blinks, then lets out a soft laugh, shaking her head.

**Maria:** Oh, I didn't mean it like that! I just meant the minibar is stocked with drinks and snacks—complimentary, as in free. Not that I don't find you charming, of course.

Alex chuckles, pushing off the desk and straightening up.

**Alex:** Well, that's a shame. I was already imagining the kind of 'complimentary' services you might offer. Guess I'll just have to make do with the mini liquor bottles and overpriced chocolate.

Maria rolls her eyes, but her smile is warm.

**Maria:** I'm sure you'll survive. If you need anything else, just let me know. We're here to help—within reason.

Alex winks, turning towards the elevators.

**Alex:** Within reason, huh? Sounds like a challenge. I'll be sure to test those limits.
